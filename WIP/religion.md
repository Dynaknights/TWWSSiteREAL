The world's largest religion (~3.1 billion worldwide followers) is **Neo-Panpsychism** (sometimes called **Panpsychism** or just **Psychism**), a complicated <small>kinda-sorta</small> **polytheistic**/**pantheistic** system of beliefs centered around a 'cosmic being,' the Universe itself, and everything within it being a byproduct of its desire to create a 'perfect story' as a means of escapism from itself and the world around it. Thus, most of Neo-Panpsychism also revolves around stories and storytelling as concepts.


## The Universe

[The Universe](/outerspace) (sometimes referred to as the **Stars**, **Our Glory**, or maybe even **Eternity**/**Infinity**) is what it says on the tin. It's not just outer space, it's the embodiment of *everyone* and *everything* in the most literal sense. It's the yellowing leaves falling from autumn trees and the wide-eyed rabbits, the people on public transport and the street performers with hats on the ground, you and me. It's often seen as the ultimate coalescence of the **goodness** and **love** it's bestowed upon its creations.  
This also means that — by human standards — the Universe is simultaneously horrific. It's slaughtered people and animals alike, poisoned itself beyond measure, committed atrocities the human psyche can't even fathom, making the Cosmos also the pinnacle of **evil** and **wretchedness**. While Our Glory's love and hatred are represented as a dichotomy — that being two separate entities known as the **Stars Above** and **Stars Below** respectively — it's understood that they're simply facets of one being.

The Universe is also sometimes dubbed **Firmament**. However, this title is usually reserved for *before* its brain breaks and it creates everything, for lack of better words.

## Values

Recurring themes include (but aren't limited to) stories, storytelling, the writing process, consequence, the human experience, love, understanding (understanding themselves, people, media, the world, etc.). Major, major, *major* emphasis on **good versus evil**, **stories**, **wishes**, and **fate**.  
These themes may not be present or hold much importance as others across individuals and denominations since these groups may honor different gods/aliens/mythical creatures/etc. along with their values, morals, and stories.

## The Creation Gist

When life and everything was null and void, there existed only one being: the **Firmament**. When it reached unmeasurable heights of its despair after lonesome eons, it yearned for a means of escaping its inherent wretchedness. Then, a grand idea was born! It would create a **perfect story** encapsulating all of its hopes and dreams so that it could be anyone but itself, a perfect paradise: something people call **the Grand Scheme of Things**.  

After uncountable eternities <small>(haha)</small>, a once blank plane of existence was now populated with matter and energy that stretched as far as the eye could see. Freshly birthed planets eagerly awaited to be gifted their respective lifeforms; millions and billions and trillions of intertwined little stories all in one grand design just begging to be put to paper!  
The Firmament looked upon its fantastical reality… and wept, for it was not what it had envisioned. Not in the slightest. Each micrometer of the plane reeked of careless errors and cracks and inconsistencies and foulness and wrongness and— *this wasn't the Firmament's hopes and dreams*. No, no, no no no no no *no*. It cannot be. It *would not* be.

So, everything was torn down to be birthed anew. Again, and again, and again. These are what are titled **universal iterations**/**cycles**.  
While the creation process was polished more and more with each iteration, it was evident that the canvas was also deteriorating at the same rate: natural laws failed to govern, the skies were painted the wrong hues, things abruptly came into and out of existence. What's worse is that every patchwork fix seemed to tear another more scathing hole elsewhere.  
This Grand Scheme… seemed to be catastrophic, evidently. The Firmament's screams permeated every blazing star, barren planet, and the spaces between.

Thus, here we are in this current iteration: what's now referred to as the **Seventh Cosmos**, which is even more laden with anomalies than the others.  
The Firmament's mind and soul was already haunted by its many, many failures, mind you. So, seeing the Seventh Cosmos be such an inherent *disappointment* served as a wake-up call. Their savior of a perfect story — a Grand Scheme — would never be realized.

Grief-stricken, heartbroken, and agonized, the Firmament's mind literally broke beneath the strain of it all, becoming everyone and everything all at once, for this Grand Scheme is the only thing it knows and the only thing that matters. Soon, it'll all return to the void of infinite possibilities whence it came.

## Millions and Billions and Trillions of Gods!?

It's no secret that Neo-Panpsychism's mythology and legends have an absurd number of alien races, abstract entities, and other legendary or cosmic beings just… hanging around. Devout followers tell of multiverses, of beings *outside* of said multiverses, of new forces that govern the observable universe, magic, humanity's encounters and battles with cosmic beings, with each of these elements seemingly changing on a whim as years pass and those retcons just being accepted at some point.  
Well, the people love it. Maybe that's all that matters.

Anywho, Neo-Panpsychic lore is intimidating and ever-changing, with new gods and beings seemingly spun up from nothingness for the sake of a cool story or… *plot convenience*.

In fact, all gods — even from different religions — are considered real or 'canon' by Neo-Panpsychics. As these individuals have a drive to understand others and the world at large, many have taken it upon themselves to intermingle with different people to study their cultures, resulting in a lot of syncretism among these religious groups. So much so, that Neo-Panpsychism just has… an absolutely ludicrous number of gods, supernatural beings, and mythical creatures 'borrowed' from different mythos.  
…But doesn't this sound conflicting and overly-convoluted? It is. More than you think, really. With how intricate this all sounds, it's no wonder Neo-Panpsychics demanded at least [a week specifically dedicated to studying the lore and nothing else](/world/holidays#Storytide).

There are some Panpsychics who consider everything listed to be a matter of mere tall tales, believing that Eternity's the only 'real' being. Generally, people will either believe in a chosen handful of beings or most of them; the most popular of which being a group of abstract entities known as the **Ur divisors**: Eternity/Infinity, Death, Master Order, Lord Chaos, Oblivion, The-Powers-That-Be, The-Natural-Order-of-Things, and the Living Tribunal.  
Other lesser great beings, such as Lady Death, Mistress Love, Sire Hate, the Queen of Nevers, Galactus, the Beyonders, so on and so forth receive their fair shares of worship, stories, and attention.  

…But for the sake of my well-being and redundancy, I'm not going to go into depth on who these are.
