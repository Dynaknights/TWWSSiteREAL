**Marie-Julie Watson** should get her life together. You know it as much as she does.  
Shit's never that simple though, let's be real.

Once upon a time, life used to hinge around hiding and scurrying about, as though she were a mouse. **MJ**'s life used to be full of **fear**. There's no other word that's so picture-perfect.  
Fearing the hand of her father, fearing loving her mother too much for her own good, fearing the roof over her head would cave in once and for all, putting all of them out of their misery.  Hiding, avoiding, and hiding again. There was nothing else.

She wishes she could say these early years blurred together into a haze, but MJ's not gonna lie to you. Instead, they still seem to jump out of their respective chapters to gnaw at her mind with blinding clarity.  
The weird thing is that the right to be shrouded by impermeable fog was actually reserved for her *adulthood*; the *now*. Funny how that works.  
Since moving away from that stars-forsaken household in search of a better life — a life true to herself and not gripped day-in-day-out by fear — they've found that misery really does love company. Of course, they hadn't believed the lasting effects would *magically disappear* after they drove all the way to the East Coast, no, but this aimless, lifeless stupor they're stuck in hits far, far harder than her father ever could.

You know the feeling; you wake up, do what people tell you to do, and — next thing you know — you're vomiting into the nearest toilet in a tight black dress with deafening music blasting from the other room while scared shitless because you abruptly remembered a childhood memory that you were stupid enough to believe you could bury.  
A hand is stiffly and awkwardly patting your back, belonging to someone who has your lipstick on their neck, but whose name and visage you can't recall for the life of you. Your bank account is somehow emptier than the day before even though you're trying your hardest.

Rinse and repeat, and you'll have found yourself becoming the perfect party girl. It's really not as hard as people believe.

MJ should be happy. They should be better off than they were in the Midwest, at least.  
They're not destined to be some party girl. She's supposed to be independently living her best life surrounded by people who'll support her with every step of the way. She shouldn't be consumed by this stars-damned ***fear***. Her final resting place shouldn't be some random guy's dirty couch.

MJ should get her life together.
