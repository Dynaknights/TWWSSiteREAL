### Equipment
- **Two bio-engineered squid-like tentacles**  
Long, slimy, fleshy, smelly. The tips have club-like structures with hooks on their undersides. Used for heavy manhandling and heavy hitting.
- **Two bio-engineered squid-like arms**  
Wiggly appendages with hooks trailing down the undersides. Used for locomotion and smacking.  
Neither the arms nor tentacles are mechanical; they grew 'naturally' out of her back with a technique Lucille refers to as "**Chemical Coercion**," in which Lucille implanted a 'blueprint' into her genes, making her body believe it needs to grow the given parts. This process is artificially accelerated and can be regenerated.
