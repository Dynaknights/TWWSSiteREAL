Well, summer break was exactly as boring as I anticipated.  
I played some games, browsed some websites, read some books, listened to some music, and watched a lot of Vinesauce while I did other things because I will be incapacitated if left alone with my thoughts for a significant amount of time. Yup.  
Oh, and I worked on this site/*TWWS*, too, of course, but it's mostly just been working or sitting on my ass. I mean literally just sitting around because while I have a lot of things I need or want to do, I struggle immensely with mustering up the motivation to actually engage with said things… Which is also why Art Fight burnt me the fuck out and I had to peace out earlier than I planned Sigh…

Or maybe it's the lack of socialization I had during summer break that's fucking me over here. I dunno. I haven't really left my room if I wasn't going to my kitchen or to work since I got back. Not that there's many places in my unwalkable ass hometown to venture to besides the local mall and comic book store, anyhow! Shit's miserable, man.  
I suppose I could also talk to people online, but **1)** no social media, **2)** evil curse of low spoons, and **3)** bad social skills…

Anyway, enough moping. I bought an original Nintendo 3DS off eBay (why the hell are these things more expensive than PS4s bundled with controllers, HDMIs, *and* games?) because why not?  
I didn't realize that a region-free console meant it would be pre-modded and was kind of disappointed but like hey, whatever. I didn't know shit about Nintendo consoles beyond the N64 because I've just never been very interested in their games until recently. Also because I've only owned a grand total of one console. Ever. So, don't hammer me for my incompetence here.  
It took around three weeks to ship plus an extra week or two because I forgot to get an SD card reader in advance. Smooth sailing from there though.

Modding the thing was, indeed, pretty easy (I just followed [3ds.hacks.guide](https://3ds.hacks.guide/)). I thought that it was just some condescending meme whenever 3DS modders said hacking it was easy but no, it was just 'download this file and drag it to this folder' with the occasional 'choose this option.'  
I honestly don't know what I expected but I thought I would fail spectacularly.

Also, yes, there are games on it (before I'm called a LARPer/poser…). I don't own any physical games because I didn't want to buy more shit so I downloaded whatever tickled my fancy on the hShop. My WiFi's slow and I'm unused to having to work with such a small amount of storage space — I typically download a bunch of ROMs and then organize them by system and genre — but it's whatever.  
Currently, I've got: *Animal Crossing: New Leaf*, *LEGO Marvel Avengers*, *Pokémon Y*, and *Mario Kart 7*. I intend to try more games, of course; mainly SNES/GBA stuff since I kept losing my goddamn save files whenever I swapped computers (I'm now a bit better at file management), thus I never finished anything… but just give me some time to actually play said games first.  
I also tried *Tomodachi Life*. However, managing my Miis stressed me the FUCK out for whatever reason, so it was banished. Also because it's very repetitive. Oh well… It was fun-ish while it lasted. Not really but kinda. Maybe *Living the Dream*'s better? But I'll have to hold off until I get something that can run it.  
Despite *Tomodachi Life*'s faults, it was nice seeing everyone within *TWWS* being happy and carefree and befriending everyone.

Except Sasha. Can someone fucking get her out of there. Why is she there. How'd she even get in?
