I'm alive… unfortunately. Wish I wasn't.  
School is boring as hell. Work is literal hell. I'm lonely as hell, but I don't have the energy or motivation to try befriending my peers. I feel as though I'm wasting my life away on things I feel as though I must want to do but can't definitively gauge whether or not that's true due to poor self-appraisal. If I think about my life retrospectively or the state of this world in any lens for more than five seconds, I will explode into bloody giblets. 2026 is almost over.  
This shit is so ass, but I'm trying to make the best of it or whatever.

Anyway, *TWWS*… I haven't been able to do much with it because I've been busy with life obligations and engaging with random media. Or, y'know, sitting on my ass. I think I said somewhere that I sometimes get extremely obsessed with certain things and have them spiral (like the creation of this website). Yeah. It just seems to be video games instead of superheroes and comics at the moment.  
I've mainly been playing 3DS games (mostly *Pokémon Y* and *Mario Kart 7*, along with *Super Mario 3D Land*), but also slowly progressing through N64 titles. My time with N64 titles has significantly improved after I swapped out my DualShock-ish controller for an N64 one with a USB connector. Now, I can actually enjoy playing these games without having a coronary! Yay! The camera's still weird to me because I'm spoiled by modern quality of life improvements, but I'll adapt, eventually. Keep in mind that this system predates me by like, a lifetime.

Speaking of the N64… I've been watching the *Nintendo Power* promo and Nintendo retailer VHS tapes recently — because I guess this is my life now — and I even have all of the *Nintendo Power* magazines downloaded as PDFs on my computer <small>(issue #49's cover… the stuff of fucking nightmares, man)</small>, which got me thinking…  
I don't use (mainstream) social media aside from watching Vinesauce VODs, listening to music, looking at awful pictures of me my friends post, or talking to other friends on — \*gags* — Discord, right? Plus, I enjoy playing these games as 'authentic-ish' as possible (with what little I have, anyway). Why not try beating a game completely blind with only *Nintendo Power* magazines for help, considering I can't dial a Nintendo Game Counselor any time soon? The N64 did release thirty years (and a day) ago, after all! Also because I want to like, read the actual issues themselves.  
I suppose it sounds pretty banal now that I'm putting my mind to paper (or… keyboard?), but I've gotta have fun somehow in this crumbling economy. It's not like I'm buying a Switch 2 or Steam Deck anytime soon. If it's really as bland as it sounds, then I'll probably pivot to playing spooky games for October instead.

**2) Neo-Panpsychism**

This term's probably been thrown around on the site somewhere and I've made some possibly pretty subtle allusions to real-world religions not being as prominent ('Universe' is typically capitalized, expletives, URL's motto, whatever). They still exist, but Panpsychism here basically took Abrahamic religions' number one spot on beliefs most subscribed to.  
I know it *was* on [Nouvelle-Yorke](/world/vny)'s info table listed as it's predominant religion up until some point recently, but I've never gotten around to making a page for it because, well, it's still shaky to me and I only really have vague ideas of what I want it to be. Hell, I'm still unsure on whether or not to rename it.

Anyway, at its very basics (at the time of writing and could change), Neo-Pansychism is a multi-theistic belief about storytelling.  
Yes, storytelling. I guess you could call it an advanced storytelling fandom that literally became a religion. I'm very much not religious in the slightest and am horrible at writing characters who are due to my inexperience, but I do like stories and have found that storytelling is a pretty important aspect in many beliefs and the human experience as a whole, so why not just emphasize that importance and give it a spotlight?

Also, I love this video called [The ENTIRE Marvel Multiverse Cosmology: the History, the Gods, the Structure, and Beyond](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_S_hpLrI5YM) by Sir_Superhero (good video, good channel, watch him) and I realized I could just… squeeze Marvel's 616 cosmology into Neo-Panpsychism.  
I was initially planning on not touching Marvel's cosmic beings with a ten-foot pole because it's just such an insane can of worms (that's several decades worth of comic history!) and TWWS is just street-level nonsense. But… Marvel's cosmology has an emphasis on narratives, both making them and engaging with them… And Neo-Panpsychism's been kind of underbaked since I made it… So, I'll try my best to rework it from the ground up. Again.
