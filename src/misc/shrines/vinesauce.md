Hi. This is the rambly and half-assed page dedicated to the only two reasons I go on YouTube anymore aside from listening to music playlists and spontaneously watching random video essays when I'm bored.

Like, I'm dead serious. **Vinny** and **Joel** are the only real people (aside from some game developers) I'd say I'm an avid fan of nowadays.  
I've never cared about celebrities(?? Is that the right term? I guess so, right?) beyond funny internet people playing video games since I was a kid, so maybe my enjoyment is just the newest iteration of this trait. Not to mention that I absolutely ***DESPISED*** getting out of my comfort zone and trying new things until recently. The desire to discover new 'content creators' is a nonzero number, but *preeeetty* damn close to zero.

## Whomst?

**Vincent "Melpert/Binyot" Pizzapasta**, otherwise known as **Vinny Vinesauce**, is an American Twitch streamer/YouTuber who is — at the time of writing — forty-one-years-old, but mentally four. He makes funny jokes sometimes on witch.tv and I enjoy tuning into him playing/corrupting weird old games. He also spins sometimes.

Vinny's also a co-founder of the rock band **Red Vox**, which I suppose would technically be my favorite band, considering I've always cared more about the art rather than the artist when it comes to music. Until now. Funny how that works.  
Anyway, my favorite album of theirs is ***Retcon***, with ***The New Flesh*** and ***Reminds Me…*** being my top two favorite songs on there.

**Joel "Jobel/Yoey" Johansson**, otherwise known as **Vargskelethor** or **Joel Vinesauce**, is a giggly Swedish boy live streamer with a fecal quality sense of humor that eventually and inevitably rubbed off on me. He vaguely reminds me of a mix of Lancer and Susie from *Deltarune*, but has a higher chance of rendering you deaf.

I'd say Joel is my all-time favorite streamer/content creator. Ever. Is that weird to say? It could just be my excitement in the moment taking over and possessing me… haunting me, like a ghost. I've had a history of latching onto funny people playing video games and losing my mind over them for a bit, but I don't think it's ever been *this* bad.  
I only either listen to this dude's shitpost music on repeat or his streams while at work, his streams or music is on as background noise while I'm commuting, cooking, working on assignments, drawing, playing games, working on this very website… I'm so unfathomably cooked.  
I don't even think we'd get along if he was just some guy I met in real life! I dunno what my problem is…

I dunno. I dunno! I'm usually a pretty verbose dude, but my words and thought process are falling apart because I don't know how to articulate how much I enjoy watching this guy make stupid jokes while playing video games. Just know I made this shrine in a frenzy and it feels like my mind was briefly taken over by some malicious Vinesauce entity.

Joel's also a co-founder of the thrash metal band **Scythelord**. However, I'm more of a rock and heavy metal guy, so I'm gonna do a U-turn and say that Joel's high effort shitpost songs are frustratingly good and I hate them.  
I decided to sit down and listen to the album ***Faster Than Sex*** a few days ago (at the time of writing, it'd been released for like, four months). The whole *hour and forty-five minutes*. Unfortunately, I enjoyed it and still find it funny. My favorites on there are ***Euro(b)meat***, ***Sex With Bulldozer***, and ***Carheist***. Fuck my stupid baka life.

Anyway, happy we live at the same time so I can watch his videos and streams. :) Love watching this joyous man.

## How?

Let me start by saying I vaguely knew of That One Granddad Meme's existence because it popped up in an SMG4 video(?) I watched when I was a very little kid, but my reaction was more like, "What the fuck is this. Why the hell does Mario look like that," because I didn't know what the hell a Vinesauce was.

So, let's fast-forward.  
Sometimes, I feel the need to check up on old interests of mine so that I can experience them over and over again, like some gas station crackhead who touches you on Tuesdays. One day in **2022**, I guess I was in a nostalgic mood, because I was browsing YouTube for let's plays of ***Jazzpunk***, as it'd been released when my age was in the single digits (probably around a year or two older than when I saw Granddad for the first time, actually!) and I hadn't seen it since the DLC released a few years prior (over EIGHT YEARS AGO at the time of writing. Jesus Christ).
I remember I had watched IHasCupQuake and Jacksepticeye play this game forever ago, but I kept seeing these blue and green mushrooms show up in the search results, so I was like, "Alright, fine," and now my soul will never enter Heaven.

I watched Joel's let's play first. I thought he was a very funny dude whose accent I couldn't pinpoint for the life of me because I don't pay attention to Europe outside of France for my nefarious purposes. Anyway, he said something like, "…This is like a game Vinny would play, where you play as, like, a triangle, think so hard you shit, and then the game's over."  
Teenage me thought it was *sooooooooo* funny. So I subscribed to his YouTube channel immediately upon hearing this stupid line and then checked out Vinny's channel to verify this for myself before subscribing to him, too. Especially since they both seem to enjoy weird and/or old games, which meshed perfectly with my tastes (I'm not a Nintendo guy, but hey, still)!

…And now I'm here. Please help me it's been four years and I have a bachelor's in male-pattern baldness and a minor in Fecal Funny. God help me.
