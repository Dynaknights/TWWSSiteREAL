Howdy. <img src="/assets/decor/myMelo1.gif">

My name is **Kirimu**. **Machine** works fine, too. Nice to meet you.

I consider myself to be a talkative recluse. I'm also black, aroace, and transmasculine… if that matters. I dunno, I just got here.  
Sometimes I like certain things too much and they spiral; this website's a good example of that. No, I'm not proud of myself, but I guess I'll see this site through to its end.

*The Wondrous Web-Surfer* is a headworld-paracosm-thing that's existed since **2023**, which is pretty recent in terms of paracosms, hence why there's not a lot of stuff on this site yet; I'm working on it and dealing with adulthood. Kind of a lot of stuff going on at the minute and that makes updates infrequent. I'm sorry about that.  
I created this site out of sheer impulse; I wanted to make a personal website and practice web dev, but I'm a pretty boring person overall and couldn't figure out what my site would be about, so I figured, *"Why not have the thing revolve around the world in my head?"* This site exists to keep track of and archive things relating to *TWWS* and all of the other paracosms within it. Does it accomplish that effectively? No fucking clue.

Currently, I'm working toward my **electrical engineering bachelor's** (plus maybe an **art minor** in the future) and begrudgingly doing other adult things with my time.  
I don't use social media anymore, so most of my free time is spent chilling. Playing video games, watching Vinesauce, drawing, reading, web surfing, looking for critters under rocks… and working on the site, too. Yeah.

## Web-Surfer?

This might sound weird, but *TWWS* is one of very few things that I give a fuck about currently.  
It's difficult to actually articulate that, since a lot of what comprises this paracosm in its entirety isn't up on the site and probably will never be, but it's like a pet roach I can't get rid of, I suppose. 

*TWWS* is weird. My relationship with it is weird. It's one of the most self-indulgent and personal things I've ever created — as with many daydreams and paracosms, I'm sure. Is it me or a part of me? I don't know. Regardless, I'm happy to talk about *TWWS* at length to (almost) anyone who asks, but I might be like… weird and aggro and protective over it? Especially with specific themes or characters? My characters and stories are a reflection of myself/my life one way or another, so I'm sorry in advance if I take something to heart. I can't really help it.
