The **end of the third millennium** had been the muse of dreams for centuries. A seemingly mythical point in time; a promise waiting to come to fruition thanks to the working class, especially during the millennium. People placed their hopes and wishes in the pursuit of happiness; a better future.  
Upon glimmering podiums and tech summits, the future was salvation and it was both the civic duty and divine obligation of all to make it as best it can be. Time is a machine powered by the beating hearts of you and I — the indomitable human spirit.

Now, in the pastel-hued era of the **2990s**, tomorrow is today; and today… is yesterday. And the day before that, and the day before that, as with every day before and after.

Of course, there are some improvements: climate change is history, robots didn't take over the world, flora lines every corner and edge of previously drab cities, and average day-to-day lives are — by 21st century standards — excitedly larger-than-life courtesy of the presence of supers.  
However, the same eternal problems still bubble beneath the eternal status quo, the same eternal ignorance to problems that only affect the other, the same eternal desperation for power and control; how humanity hasn't cannibalized itself out of existence is anyone's guess!

It feels as though nothing's changed. Maybe nothing ever will.

## The United Republic of Leathucaria
<div class="box4boxes" style="max-height:60em;">

<img src="/assets/decor/stars.gif" alt="A GIF of colorful, twinkling stars." aria-hidden="true"> *Main article: [United Republic of Leathucaria](/world/url)*

The **United Republic** is a country that is totally, most indubitably, 100% ***NOT*** the **United States** at all whatsoever, thank you very much. They are two entirely separate entities, with the latter no longer existing.

Unlike the States in its wretched, writhing *chaos*, the Republic is a proud and collected nation. It's much more stable than the hellhole of America could ever be, thanks to the hard work of its diligent people and the astute minds of its impeccable leaders, who are nothing but dedicated to striving toward the future in the name of **starlight and prosperity**!

### Ville-de-Nouvelle-Yorke

<img src="/assets/decor/stars.gif" alt="A GIF of colorful, twinkling stars." aria-hidden="true"> *Main article: [Ville-de-Nouvelle-Yorke](/world/vny)*

A city that never sleeps, yet always dreams.  
Like most dreams, everything happens, but nothing changes <small>(for the better, anyway; everything somehow worsens with each passing day)</small>.

**La Pomme Grosse** — like the rest of human society — is ferociously gripped by the intensity of its own spectacle. Despite the strange history that haunts both Nouvelle-Yorke and the United Republic, both everything and nothing feels notably different when comparing 2990s Nouvelle-Yorke to 1990s New York; seems like all of its bumbling inhabitants are stuck in a temporal limbo of their own making while waiting, waiting, and waiting for something to happen.

Also, Nouvelle-Yorke simultaneously has the highest amount of **supers** in the world, but also has the one of the highest **crime rates**. Does anyone even care anymore?
