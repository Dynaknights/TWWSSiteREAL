**January 1st, 2800** marks the day the United States of America — after reclaiming power from the French — disavowed its roots and pledged to march forward, leaving its history and French takeover behind them.  
The American president at the time, **Corduroy Centauri**, stated that the avarice, loneliness, megalomania, corruption, and monumental failures haunting the States would see their absolute ends as the great country liberated itself from self-made shackles under a new name: **the United Republic of Leathucaria**.

It should be noted that *'Leathucaria'* is a meaningless word. Even over a century after it's introduction, it still lacks a proper definition, as its creator has never gifted it one. People are unsure if Centauri simply forgot or if it, too, is a part of Leathucaria's hodge-podge philosophy.  
The word has never existed before it left Centauri's mouth — much to the confusion and shock of the audience <small>(but maybe that's due to the previous President being shot dead on stage)</small> — because *'Leathucaria'* came to him whilst sleeping on the prior night of New Year's Eve.  
Specifically, the President stated, *"I dreamed a rosy, sunkissed dream of a grandest future; a complete and utter disassociation from a past built upon cruel, needless suffering in the name of pseudo-godhood for the few! A grandest future crafted from the blood, sweat, and tears of a country whose people joined hand-in-hand and unified beneath the loving banner of **starlight and prosperity**. Does that not sound like a perfect New Year's dream?"*  
The name change did not pass without a fight, but was reluctantly accepted into the collective consciousness as time went on, as most things transpire.

Whether or not Centauri's dream came to pass has recently been a heated debate within the modern day, with many speculating the legitimacy of Centauri's alleged dream, wondering if he'd simply *lied* (and possibly plagiarized) using propaganda techniques from his forefathers. The nonsensical, superficial air surrounding the country has led others to ask if this is truly what it's like to be in a dream; disoriented, nebulous, and trapped in a sort of stasis.

Leathucaria is a proud superpower, constantly boasting its innovations and quality of life, along with how much progress has been made since Europeans first stepped onto its soil. However, the denizens say otherwise; everything happens all at once, but **nothing ever changes**.  
The Republic is certainly a dream, but it's never been the people's.

### French/American Lean
The **French/American Lean** (FrAm lean) most often refers to the proportion of Leathucarian French to American English spoken regularly in any given state by its denizens during and after France's occupation of the states, but this term can also be used to describe the amount of French culture in general present in a place. This can range from a state near-exclusively speaking either language or, more commonly, adopting a mix of both. The FrAm lean can't be represented as a percentage because culture cannot be quantified, so it's just stated which language/culture has the most influence in an area.

Large and dense metropolises generally have more french influence within them than smaller cities and rural areas because France had aimed to subject as many people to its propaganda as possible during its ruling, so America's hotspots were hit the hardest with this cultural shift. This resulted in people from said rural and smaller places sticking with the pre-Empty Eras American culture while larger cities tend to seem more French.

### Leathucarian French
**Leathucarian French** (otherwise known as Elderberry French, Eagle-Fucker/Kisser French, or Bullshit French) is a questionable [variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variety*(linguistics)) of French. It currently stands as the second most prominent variety after standardized Leathucarian English and has wormed its way from its origins of hatred to apathetic day-to-day living.

It's a purposefully chaotic and confusing mess of an isolect erected upon the mountains of spite people — primarily minorities with an emphasis on black people — harbored for France's coup d'etat on the United States (2600s to 2700s).  
The story goes that people considered more worthless within the social hierarchy — those stuck in laborious blue-collar jobs with low wages and little upward mobility — developed a code to communicate regarding disrupting France's tyrannical rule while remaining inconspicuous. This was initially done by intentionally breaking the rules of standardized French and mixing it with other languages. However, in the modern day, this kind of just devolved back into textbook French due to France's vice grip on the country, even long after its rule was overthrown. The French spoken in places like Paris or Lyon is prestigious and grand to Leathucarians, hence why the revolutionary aspect of the variety was overlooked.

Despite the admiration for Parisian French, there's quite a few key differences: Leathucarian French sounds more robotic, spoken Leathucarian French speakers use a strange midpoint between an American and French accent/tone — even when speaking English, simpler/'dumbed down' words are used more often, gendering nouns depends on the chosen presentation of the *speaker* rather than relying on memorization, gender-neutral terms exist along with more leeway in terms gender presentation, people are fine with throwing in Anglicized words either because no French translation exists or because they forgot the name for something, etc.  
Despite its origins and French people's distaste for Leathucarian French, a Leathucarian French and another Francophone speaker should have little-to-no issues with general understanding if they avoid words unique  to their respective varieties.

### Comité Fédéral des Loisirs

In every state exists at least one ***Comité Fédéral des Loisirs*** (CFL)*: a government service which plans, sets up, and maintains leisurely social clubs and activities for a given populace. The group was established 2800 in response to a debate overtaking Leathucaria's brand new government after the fall of the United States. 
The United Republic's first Vice President, **Mercybelle Kehlani**, had angrily proposed, *"Why not establish a starsdamned National Mandatory Fun Council to enforce fun and whimsy throughout the country? It's perfect, especially since the majority of the populace is still too overworked and underpaid trying to keep themselves alive to actually have fun. You won't fail spectacularly at all. It'll go smoothly, I'm sure."*

The President at the time, **Corduroy Centauri**, failed to notice her *entire* sentence was sarcasm — not just the latter part. Kehlani's suggestion was taken with pride and thus, the CFL was founded, much to her own dismay.

Much to the newborn government's surprise, however, the CFL was positively received in the cities it debuted in. More federal funds were allocated for the organization to expand.

CFL activities are large in scope and can be just about anything: group pumpkin carving nights, stargazing with astronomy trivia, sci-fi space opera romantasy book clubs, symposiums about how puppetry can be an effective mode of storytelling, nude model live drawing, farmers' markets, picnics, circuses with animal holograms, ballet performances, etc. Anyone can also arrange with the organization to set up their own event for little-to-no cost, such as concerts and fairs.  
The reason CFL is so widely successful is because all its activities are either free or affordable regardless of how expensive they were to arrange. They give people free treats and free things to do with free materials; all that's asked for is participation (and completing a survey). Considering the chaotic economic state of Leathucaria, its denizens didn't object to the CFL's establishment.
